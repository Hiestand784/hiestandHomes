Sellers

Many people are not aware of how much goes into selling a house. Preperation alone can seem daunting, but taking things one step at a time will streamline the process. Learn more about selling a house below:

Steps to Successfully Sell Your House:

First impressions matter. Having your house clean, orderly, and presentable will draw more potential buyers during showings

  • Consider small repairs that will be cost effective
  • Enhance curb appeal with landscaping, a few flowers can go a long way
  • Remove as many personal belongings as possible so the next buyer can envision themselves in the home

Setting the right price is critical for getting attention and keeping your house from stalling on the market

  • Understand the current market situation by rese
  • Consult a real estate professional for a true market analysis, click here to get in touch with me easily!
  • Be realistic about your home's value, setting a price too high can cause a house to stall seem undesirable, while setting it too low leaves money on the table
  • Work with your agent to get the house listed and maintain the house in a "ready to show" state

Having your house ready for a showing at any time will increase your chances of finding a buyer. While it doesn't need to be in perfect shape 24/7, having it ready to show on short notice is ideal

  • Keep your house ready to show on short notice, and keep a line of contact open to schedule showings
  • Prepare for showings and open houses by double checking that the place is as inviting as possible to the next buyer
  • Anticipate negotiating price across multiple or few offers, keeping in mind that this is not necessarily the set final price
  • Know that after you and a buyer agree to a price, there are a few more steps remaining

After accepting an offer there are a few final steps to be taken to complete the deal

  • Double check with your agent that you are fulfilling your contractual agreements that may include inspection, repairs, or other contingencies
  • Work with your agent to ensure all paperwork is in order and signed
  • Be prepared for the buyer to do a final walk through of the house to ensure the house is in proper condition
  • Sign the closing papers and paying closing costs
