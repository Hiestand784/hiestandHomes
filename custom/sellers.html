Sellers

Selling a home entails many steps, more than some people realize. My job as a real estate agent may include: running a Competitive Market Analysis (CMA), finding the right price, staging the home to maximize appeal, getting professional pictures taken for marketing, listing the home through Multiple Listing Services (MLS), scheduling showings, and guiding you through the closing process. It may seem like a lot, but I am here to make everything as simple as possible!

My goal is to sell your home in a timely manner for the highest return possible. I will break down the process into understandable terms and guide you through each decision. I once flew back mid-vacation to help a client through closing, that is my level of commitment!

Let's work together to get your home sold for the best price:

For an overview of selling a home, see my guide below:

Steps to Successfully Sell Your Home:

First impressions matter. Having your home clean, orderly, and presentable is critical for attracting as many buyers as possible. Keeping your house presentable throughout the selling process is an ongoing effort. Getting rid of clutter will help buyers envision themselves in the home, and also prepare your future moving efforts. When many houses have similar features, attention to detail will make your home stand out.

As a realator I have many connections that can help you with these following steps:

  • Clean the inside and outside of your home, paying attention to often overlooked places (gutters, shutters, window sills)
  • Remove personal belongings to increase buyer appeal
  • Ask me about hiring a stager and photographer to prepare your home for listing
  • Consider small repairs, an inexpensive fix may be worth more in property value
  • Enhance curb appeal with landscaping, some simple flowers can go a long way

The staging process is critical and hard to capture with a short overview. Remember that I will be there to help you through this step with many tips and tricks, ready to answer any questions you have.

Setting the right price is critical for getting attention in a competitive market. My job is to help you sell your home for the best price in a timely manner. A Competitive Market Analysis (CMA) helps us set a price to maximize the return from selling your home.

We can work together to:

  • Understand current market trends
  • Complete a Competitive Market Analysis (CMA)
  • Set the right price for your home in the current market
  • List your home through the Multiple Listing Service (MLS)
  • Maintain a presentable state for showings and begin marketing your home

With preperations complete, we can choose among many options to market your home. I can create flyers, mailers, signs, digital listings, social media ads, and more! I am a member of two real estate boards which puts me in contact with a wider network of agents. In addition I am a host for ADTV, a nationally acclaimed 2-time Emmy nominated TV show, which promotes listings. I can use my connections to market your home to a very wide audience. My years of experience have put me in touch with people all across Indiana, making me a valuable resource in marketing your home.

These are some of the steps we may take to market your home:

  • Get in contact with my network of resources
  • Get professional staging to draw out your home's true potential
  • Have a professional photographer show off your home from all angles
  • Get you home listed on the MLS
  • Market your home to get it sold for top dollar

With the home priced and marketed, buyers will want to see the home. I will work with you to schedule showings, and continue to market the home. Once a potential buyer shows interest, we will negotiate price and terms of the sale.

Before we close the sale, we will accomplish these following steps together:

  • Cast a wide net to bring in as many potential buyers as possible
  • Schedule showings while staying informed on offers and other feedback
  • Handle paperwork as offers come in and negotiations begin
  • Choose the best offer and begin the closing process

This is the final step in the process. Closing takes place through a title company. We will cover the documents together so I can answer any questions you have. One important document is the closing statement, which outlines the financial details of the sale. Once you have signed all the documents, the deal is complete and your home will be sold!

Here are some of the steps in the closing process:

  • Make sure all paperwork is in order and signed
  • Review valuation from buyer's lender
  • Pack up your belongings
  • Ensure the home is clean
  • Set final read date for utilities
  • Be prepared for the buyer to do a final walk through
  • Sign the closing papers and pay any closing costs
  • Receive the proceeds of the sale

Congratulations! You just finished selling your home.
