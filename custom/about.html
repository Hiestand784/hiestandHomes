Get To Know Me

Hi, I'm Sheri

In the summer of 2026 I will celebrate 40 years married to my wonderful high school sweetheart, Nicholas. I am the proud mother of four young men, and a loving grandmother. After receiving two master's degrees I spent over 30 years teaching. In 2019 I embarked on a new career path with realty, and loved it so much that I dedicated my full working time to it!

More than anything I love being active. For the past 10 years I have run the Indy Mini-Marathon, and plan to run again this year. I love nature and traveling too, with some travel highlights including Washington, Florida, North Carolina, New York, and Greece. From walking the cliffsides of Greece, to the bustling city life of New York City, to the serene beaches of Florida, I know the joys the perfcet setting can bring.

When I first found out about The American Dream TV, I loved how they emphasize positivity in communities. I knew then that it was a perfect opportunity to accent my real estate career. Be it houses or businesses or people, through ADTV I am now able to shine a spotlight on the great things Indiana has to show.
