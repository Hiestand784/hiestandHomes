Below is an overview to get you started on finding your next home:

What to consider when buying a home:

Getting your mortgage figured out is the first step to home ownership. I can connect you with a lender to get you moving towards your next home! Click here and I will help you find the mortgage program that is right for you.

Here are some things to keep in mind as you determine your mortgage:

  • Getting preapproved for a mortgage is the first step
  • Gather documents like pay stubs, tax returns, and bank statements
  • Plan for a down payment (often 5%-20% of property value)
  • Budget for a monthly loan payment after the purchase
  • Know that credit score can affect loan rates
  • Receive your preapproval letter in preperation for submitting offers

Knowing the key features to your dream home will streamline the searching process. My personal tip is to make a list of your must-haves, some nice-to-haves, and any deal breakers.

Here are some things to consider when choosing your home's style:

  • Understand how important location is, living close to work or school is important for many of my clients
  • Decide how many bedrooms and bathrooms you need
  • Think about layout, extra floors, or a basement can be a plus or something you want to avoid
  • Consider the non-living spaces too, yard size and outbuildings are a deciding factor for many buyers
  • Staying open and flexible may help you find a better deal financially

At this point things get exciting! With financing in place and a clear idea of what you want, I will use my expertise to match you with the perfect home.

At this point I will work with you to:

  • Make sense of current market trends and home prices
  • Share listings that fit your unique list of needs
  • Schedule tours of homes that are a good fit for you
  • Submit an offer on your behalf once the right home is found

After a price is agreed upon, there are a few final steps to complete the purchase. The closing process is not well known by many buyers, but that is where I step in. I will keep the last stretch of the process simple and stress-free as possible.
These final steps include:

  • Getting your new home inspected to ensure the condition is good
  • Having the home appraised seperately by the lender
  • Making sure all final agreements and paperwork properly reflect the sale
  • Paying for closing costs
  • Receving your keys an getting official ownership

Congratulations! You made it, and are now the official owner of your new home!
