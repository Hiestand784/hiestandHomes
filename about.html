Get To Know Me

Hi, I'm Sheri

In the summer of 2026, I will celebrate 40 years of marriage with my wonderful high school sweetheart, Nicholas. I am the proud mother of four young men, and a loving grandmother, "Mimi". After receiving two Master's degrees, I spent over 30 years teaching. In 2019, I embarked on a new career path as a real estate agent, and loved it so much that I do it full time!

More than anything, I love being active! For the past 10 years I have run the Indy Mini-Marathon, and plan to run again this year. I love nature, traveling, and sunsets. Some of my favorite places include Washington, Florida, North Carolina, New York, and Greece. I cherish the ancient wonders of Greece, the hustle and bustle of New York City, and the sunny beaches of Florida. Many of my favorite photos are sunsets from the places I have traveled. I also love spending time with my family, and am so proud of all of my children and grandchildren. I have a passion for helping people, and I am so grateful to be able to do that through real estate.

I became a host of The American Dream to share stories about communities in a positive light. It's the perfect opportunity to meet people and network with those who set the bar high. It's a privilege to spread positivity in the places that we live in.
